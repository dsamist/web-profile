Who I Am

I'm a Cloud & Platform Engineer with 7+ years of experience building, deploying, and operating resilient infrastructure across AWS, Azure, and hybrid environments. My journey started as a System Administrator at Wema Bank in Lagos, where I developed a deep foundation in enterprise infrastructure before moving into cloud and DevOps roles.

Today, I'm part of the centralized MSP team at ARHS Group (Accenture) in Luxembourg, managing and supporting 11 client environments spanning European Commission institutions, financial services clients, and private sector organizations — ensuring high availability, operational resilience, and SLA compliance.

I hold 10 professional certifications across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and the CNCF ecosystem. I'm passionate about platform lifecycle management, Kubernetes operations, observability, and infrastructure automation at scale.
